## In the forge

`research` is how expert knowledge enters the work as evidence instead of recall. Use it the
moment a design decision rests on a claim about someone else's API, spec, or behaviour.

**Where notes land.** `docs/research/<topic>.md`. If the repo already has a research
convention, follow that instead — the upstream instruction to match existing convention wins.

**Cite or drop it.** A claim without a first-party source does not go in the file. "The docs
probably say" is not research. If the primary source cannot be found, write that down as the
finding.

**Feeds:** `brainstorm` (design decisions), `spec`, and `doubt` (an adversary with sources
beats an adversary with opinions).
