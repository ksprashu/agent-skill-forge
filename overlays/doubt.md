## In the forge

`doubt` is in-flight. It runs while a decision is still cheap to change, which is what
separates it from `review` and `prove`.

| Skill | When | Verdict on |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `doubt` | Mid-flight, before the decision hardens | A decision |
| `review` | After the change exists | A diff |
| `prove` | Before claiming completion | Evidence |

**Pair it with `research`.** A fresh-context reviewer armed with primary sources finds real
problems. One armed only with priors finds plausible-sounding ones.

**Do not let it stall the work.** The upstream "when NOT to use" list is load-bearing. Doubt
on every keystroke ships nothing.
