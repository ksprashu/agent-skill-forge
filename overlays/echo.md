## In the forge

`echo` runs before `grill`, not instead of it. It converts a ramble into something you can
interrogate; `grill` then interrogates it.

**Order.** ramble → `echo` (approved brief) → `grill` (settle what the brief left open) →
`done` (acceptance criteria).

**Where it lands.** Save the approved echo brief to `docs/understand/<slug>-brief.md`, then
let `grill` extend the same file.

**The point of the quarantine.** The separation between the user's words and the model's
inferences is the whole value. Keep that split in the saved file too. A brief that has been
flattened into one voice cannot be audited later.
