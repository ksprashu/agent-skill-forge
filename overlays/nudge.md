## In the forge

`nudge` is the one skill here aimed at the human rather than the work. It closes a
substantive answer with 2-3 questions tied to something specific in that answer, so you
interrogate the output instead of swallowing it.

**Why it belongs in the spine.** Everything else in the forge makes the agent more rigorous.
This makes you harder to fool. An agent that is right 95% of the time and never invites
scrutiny is more dangerous than one that is right 85% and does.

**Tune it with `profile`.** The questions should sit at the level this specific person works
at. Generic "have you considered the edge cases?" is friction. "AC-003 assumes the export
stays under 10k rows — where did that number come from?" is useful.

**Once per conversation.** The upstream limit is deliberate. A nudge on every message is
nagging, and nagging gets ignored.
