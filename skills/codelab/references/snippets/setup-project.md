## Setting up the Workspace (Cloud Project)

Duration: 10:00

Before we build our application, we need a Google Cloud **Project** linked to an active **Billing Account**.

> aside positive
> **Tip: Trial Billing Account**
>
> **Instructor-Led Workshop:** If you are in a workshop, use the redemption link provided by your instructor.
>
> **Self-Study:** If you are learning on your own, you can **[redeem a trial billing account](https://bit.ly/3KGxQL0)** to run the lab without a credit card. See the [Cloud Codelab Credits guide](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/cloud-codelab-credits) for step-by-step details.

### **Step 1: Activate Billing**

1.  Open your redemption link in an **Incognito Window**.
2.  Sign in with your **Personal Gmail** account.
3.  Click **Access Credits**.
4.  Confirm your email and accept the terms to activate your trial.

### **Step 2: Create Your Project**

1.  Go to the **[Create Project Page](https://console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate)**.
2.  Enter a unique **Project Name** (for example, `my-awesome-app`).
3.  In the "Billing Account" drop-down, select your **Trial Billing Account**.
4.  Click **CREATE** and wait for provisioning to complete.

### **Step 3: Verify the Billing Link**

1.  Go to the **[Linked Billing Account Page](https://console.cloud.google.com/billing/linkedaccount)**.
2.  If you see "Link a billing account", click it and ensure your **Trial Billing Account** is selected.
3.  Your Google Cloud project is now ready.

### Summary

You have created your Google Cloud Project and linked your trial billing account. Next, prepare your development environment.
