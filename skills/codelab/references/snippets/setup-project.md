## Setting up the Workspace (Cloud Project)

Duration: 10:00

Before we build our application, we need a place for it to live! In Google Cloud, that means creating a **Project** and linking it to a **Billing Account**.

> aside positive
> **Trial Billing Account**
>
> **Instructor-Led Workshop:** If you are in a workshop, use the redemption link from your instructor.
>
> **Self-Study:** If you're learning on your own, you can **[redeem a trial billing account](https://bit.ly/3KGxQL0)**. This lets you run the lab for free without a credit card!
>
> *Need detailed instructions? Raggy recommends the [Cloud Codelab Credits guide](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/cloud-codelab-credits).*

### **Step 1: Activate Billing**

Malti and Raggy will guide you through the activation process!

<img src="img/activate_billing_comic.png" alt="Malti and Raggy's guide to activating billing" width="624.00" />

1.  Open your redemption link in an **Incognito Window**.
2.  Sign in with your **Personal Gmail** account.
3.  Click the button to **Access Credits**.
4.  Confirm your email and accept the terms to activate your trial.

### **Step 2: Create Your Project**

Now, let's create your digital shop!

<img src="img/create_project_comic.png" alt="Malti and Raggy's guide to project creation" width="624.00" />

1.  Go to the **[Create Project Page](https://console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate)**.
2.  Enter a unique **Project Name** (e.g., `my-awesome-app`).
3.  In the "Billing Account" drop-down, select your **Trial Billing Account**.
4.  Click **CREATE** and wait for the magic to happen!

### **Step 3: Verify the Link**

One last check to make sure everything is connected!

<img src="img/verify_billing_comic.png" alt="Malti and Raggy's guide to verifying billing" width="624.00" />

1.  Go to the **[Linked Billing Account Page](https://console.cloud.google.com/billing/linkedaccount)**.
2.  If you see "Link a billing account", click it and ensure your **Trial Billing Account** is selected.
3.  You are now ready to build!

### Summary

Great job! You've laid the foundation by setting up your Google Cloud Project.
