## Sentence Rhythm & Flow (Anti-Staccato Rule)
- **Connected cause-and-effect sentences (18–30 words)**: Connect what a mechanism does to why it matters using `"because"`, `"whereas"`, `"so that"`, `"which means"`, or `" - "`. Never chop related clauses into robotic 8-to-12-word fragments.
