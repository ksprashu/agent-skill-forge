# Writing Principles — Clarity, Simplicity, Humanity

Distilled from Zinsser (*On Writing Well*), Strunk & White, and practical lessons from controlled languages. The goal is writing that is clear, concrete, and pleasant to read — not rigid or mechanical.

## Core Articles
- **Clarity**: Say exactly what you mean. If the idea is muddy, the sentence will be too. Ask: "What am I trying to say?" Then check: "Did I say it?"
- **Simplicity**: Strip every sentence to its cleanest components. Prefer the short, common word that does the job. Clutter is the disease.
- **Brevity**: Every word must earn its place. Cut needless words, redundant phrases, and throat-clearing. Short is usually better than long.
- **Humanity**: Write like a person talking to another person. Use "I" or "we" when natural. Let personality and confidence show without hype.

## Practical Rules
- Prefer active voice. Name who does what.
- One clear idea per sentence where practical. When two short related actions or results follow naturally, combine them with "and" rather than forcing a full stop.
- Use concrete, specific language. Prefer precise nouns and verbs over abstractions and intensifiers.
- Omit needless words: "in order to" → "to"; "due to the fact that" → "because"; "it is important to note that" → just state the fact.
- Vary sentence length a little so the prose has rhythm. Avoid long strings of identical structure.
- Keep paragraphs short enough to look inviting.
- Rewrite. The first version is rarely the cleanest.

## STE-Flavored Discipline (Light)
Borrow the useful parts without the full controlled vocabulary or word counting:
- Short, complete sentences.
- Active or imperative for instructions.
- Keep necessary words (articles, connectors) so meaning stays unambiguous.
- Avoid heavy noun clusters and stacked modifiers.
- Do not force a fixed dictionary or mathematical limits. Beauty and readability come first.

## Quality Bar
The output should be something a careful human would be happy to read and learn from. Clear, confident, free of AI tells, and recognizably in voice.
